## Problem Statement

The 2026 War in Iran has fluctuated fossil fuel prices up and down

* **The Squeeze on Capacity:** Delta Air Lines CEO Ed Bastian noted in April 2026 that as fuel prices continue to fluctuate widly, these high energy costs would likely stay indefinately. Bastian warned that sustained fuel volatility would ultimately force airlines to scale down flight capacity over the long term to maintain structural profitability.

* **The Premium Pivot & Consumer Alienation:** As airlines cut down capacity, they are aggressively reconfiguring cabins to favor high-margin premium offerings. Although this insulates carriers from price-sensitive market swings in the short-term, it actively drives away low-to-mid-income travelers. Despite shrinking their target consumer base, airlines till have no choice but to push baseline ticket costs high just to afford the massive upfront resource expenditures required for aircraft fuel and maintenance.

* **Spirit Bankruptcy:**

In order to help airlines mitigate this circumstance, I have developed this analysis tool with the intent of allowing airlines to understand what resources they need without guessing before an aircraft even touches the ground.
